SJSU iSchool Advising Support Chatbot

A student support tool for general questions about advising, course planning, graduation requirements, registration, and BS ISDA, MLIS, MARA, and MS Informatics program logistics.

How this helps

This chatbot is designed to help BS ISDA, MLIS, MARA, and MS Informatics students find answers to common advising questions more quickly. It gives general guidance, points students to official resources, and helps reduce confusion when advising offices are busy.

Fast Answers Get support for common advising questions right away.
Official Links Find important advising and university resources faster.
Student-Friendly Simple interface designed for easy use by students.
Always Available Useful outside office hours and during busy advising periods.
