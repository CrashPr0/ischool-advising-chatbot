This page gives an overview of the graduation process and the steps to apply.

Eligibility for Graduation
Before applying for graduation, verify that you meet the following criteria:
- Earned at least 85 units (90 units for some colleges), with no more than 70 units from community colleges
- Be in good academic standing (being on academic notice is fine)
- Be able to complete all degree requirements within one or two semesters
You should also:
- Confirm you have completed all GE and SJSU Studies requirements on MySJSU.
- Review all expected transfer credits. Contact eval@sjsu.edu to report any discrepancies.
- Ensure that you have declared all majors and minors.
- Verify your name, mailing address, and email address on MySJSU.
Finally, check for any major or college specific requirements.
- College of Science students must follow specific steps to apply for graduation: https://www.sjsu.edu/science-ssc/graduation/apply-to-graduate/index.php

Application Deadlines
You must apply for graduation no later than the add deadline of the term in which you wish to graduate. The add deadline is on the registration calendar: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/calendar/index.php. If you do not see the "Apply" button on MySJSU, contact your graduation evaluator: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/evaluators.php.
Be proactive. If you apply by the dates shown below, you will receive priority registration. These deadlines also apply to Graduation Date Change forms: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/graduation-date-changes.php.
Deadline 1 allows two semesters with priority registration.
Deadline 2 allows one semester with priority registration.

Planned Graduation Term: Spring 2027/ Summer 2027. Deadline 1: March 20, 2026. Deadline 2: October 9, 2026.
Planned Graduation Term: Fall 2027. Deadline 1: October 9, 2026. Deadline 2: March 19, 2027.
Earned at least 85 units (90 units for some colleges), with no more than 70 units from community colleges.
Locate and click the "Apply Button" in MySJSU.
Be sure to follow the instructions from your college to apply for graduation. Some colleges have earlier deadlines for their internal processes.
If you miss the application deadline, contact an advisor from your college success center: https://catalog.sjsu.edu/content.php?catoid=10&navoid=630.

How to Apply
Locate and click the "Apply Button" in MySJSU. Browse this tutorial for additional information: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/docs/GradAppOnlineTutorial.pdf. If you do not see the "Apply Button", contact your graduation evaluator: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/evaluators.php.
You will receive an email confirmation about your graduation status.
For additional questions at any point in the process, contact your graduation evaluator.

Graduation Status in MyProgress
Your graduation status in MySJSU should have one of five values. If your status is blank, you are not yet eligible to apply.
Eligible for Graduation: You have completed at least 85 units and have good academic standing.
Applied for Graduation: The Office of the Registrar has received your application.
On Track: You are currently on track to complete degree requirements and graduate with courses in progress.
Off Track: You have outstanding degree requirements. Review your MyProgress report for more details. This report outlines your unmet degree requirements.
Degree Awarded: Congratulations! You have completed all requirements and we have awarded your degree.

After Applying
After you have applied for graduation you will receive an email about your status.
If you have taken or are currently taking coursework at another institution, submit official transcripts that shows final grades as soon as possible. SJSU accepts official electronic transcripts. Use the etranscript@sjsu.edu email address.
Make sure each of your GPAs are 2.0 or higher: All College, SJSU Cumulative, SJSU Studies, Major, and Minor.
Review MyProgress each semester to make sure it is accurate: https://www.sjsu.edu/ue/student-resources/myprogress.php.
Resolve any I (incomplete) and RD (report delayed) grades.
Clear up outstanding Study Abroad coursework.
Clear any holds that may be on your record.
If you will not complete your degree requirements by the end of your planned semester, you must change your graduation date: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/graduation-date-changes.php.

Your Final Semester
