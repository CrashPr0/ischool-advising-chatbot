ISDA 110 - Technology, Culture, and Society
3 unit(s)
Corequisite(s): ISDA 101.
Grading: Letter Graded.
Note(s): Should be taken in the first semester of junior year.
Navigating the social, political, legal and ethical issues in data processing and use. Topics: social and political factors that interact with data mining, visualization and analytics, explainable AI and FATE (Fairness, Accountability, Transparency, and Ethics).
