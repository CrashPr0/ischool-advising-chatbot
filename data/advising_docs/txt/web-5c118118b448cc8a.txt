Apply to Graduate
To participate in Commencement, graduating students will first complete their Application to Graduate: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/application-central.php. This document is processed by graduation evaluators in the Office of the Registrar for undergraduate students and the Graduate Admissions and Program Evaluations (GAPE) office for graduate students. Once that document is approved, eligible students will receive an email invitation to register for Commencement.
As part of the registration process, graduating students will secure tickets for their guests and families. Four complimentary tickets are allotted to each graduating student. With additional tickets offered at a cost of $20 each.
This application to graduate with the Registrar (for undergraduate students) and the Graduate Admissions and Program Evaluations (GAPE; for graduate students) office is in October for graduation at the end of the fall semester and in March for graduation at the end of the spring semester.
Once you complete your application to graduate, you will then receive an invitation from Commencement to register for Commencement participation. These are two different processes. For clarity in understanding, the term "registration" herinafter will refer to your steps to participate in Commencement ceremonies and "application" to the process (with the Registrar or GAPE) needed to receive your diploma from SJSU - as mailed to you at your designated home address after Commencement.
September 28, 2026: registration period for Commencement opens.
November 20: registration period for Commencement closes. This is the last day to sign up to attend your college's Commencement ceremony.
Eligible fall 2026 graduates will receive a Commencement RSVP to their Preferred Email by September 28, 2026. Complete this process only if you desire to attend your college's Commencement ceremony.
September 28, 2026: as part of the Commencement registration process, which only graduating students may complete, four (4) complimentary tickets may be secured for families and guests.
October 23: last day to secure four (4) complimentary guest tickets.
October 26: ticket sales begin. Graduating students may secure additional tickets for $20 each.
December 11: last day for additional guest tickets sales - purchased by graduating students on behalf of their guests - in advance of Commencement.
December 16-17: in-person ticket sales at the Provident Credit Union Event Center box office.
