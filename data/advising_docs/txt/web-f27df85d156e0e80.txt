Candidacy Approval Form Information

This form is formally called the Petition for Advancement to Graduate Candidacy. It tells SJSU that your department is advancing you for review for candidacy for a graduate degree.

Students are to upload this form to the iSchool Candidacy form; do not send this form directly to the Graduate Admissions and Program Evaluations office (GAPE). Do not fax it to the iSchool.

Students do not need to have their own advisors sign this form. The iSchool will download your Candidacy Form from your submission, obtain necessary signatures, and then forward it to GAPE.

In most cases, the School of Information sets the due date prior to the GAPE due date. This is because the iSchool needs time to review the submitted form, have it signed by the iSchool Coordinator of Admissions and Academic Advising, and send it to GAPE by the posted deadline. GAPE will give the final and official approval or denial.

Due Dates for Submission to iSchool
For May graduation, submit to iSchool by September 24.
For August graduation, submit to iSchool by December 1.
For December graduation, submit to iSchool by March 25.
How to Fill Out
Follow the appropriate instructions to fill out the form online and save it to your computer for uploading to the iSchool webform, including the MLIS Candidacy Approval form instructions.
How to Submit
Digitally sign and upload your saved form to the iSchool Candidacy Form.
Once approved, do not submit this form again. To make changes, submit a substitution form.
