Bachelor of Science in Information Science and Data Analytics
Two Flexible Formats to Earn Your Bachelor’s Degree
Apply to San José State University’s BS in Information Science and Data Analytics program and gain the technical skills to turn data into strategic insight.
To support students at different stages of their academic journey, SJSU’s [School of Information](/) offers two flexible pathways to the same respected degree:
- [Four-year pathway](/bsisda-freshmen) : Earn your bachelor’s degree as a first-year or transfer student and complete a combination of online and in-person course work. This on-campus path is also an F-1 visa-eligible program.
- [100% online two-year pathway for transfer students](/bsisda-transfer-students) : Transfer your existing undergraduate credits and finish your bachelor’s degree online at SJSU.
No matter where you begin in the bachelor’s degree program, our information science and data analytics major prepares you to analyze and communicate information and play a pivotal role in decision-making in technology, business, healthcare and public service organizations.
Why Major in Information Science and Data Analytics at SJSU?
Build the technical knowledge to work with data responsibly, interpret complex information and lead the way in data-driven problem solving. In the BS in Information Science and Data Analytics program, you benefit from:
- A career-focused curriculum integrating emerging technologies and practical application, teaching you how to collect, analyze and interpret data in real-world contexts.
- Flexible program formats that allow you to progress toward your academic and professional goals in a way that fits your schedule.
- [Supportive faculty mentors](/directory-and-bios) who guide your learning and help you develop confidence working with data and technology.
- [Scholarship opportunities](/ischool-new-student-scholarships) that help reduce the cost of earning your degree.
- All iSchool students receive free admission to the [Coursera Career Academy](/coursera-career-academy) where you can earn certificates and micro-credentials online while earning your degree.

What You’ll Learn
SJSU’s information science and data analytics program delivers an immersive education at the intersection of data, technology and people. To prepare you for diverse career fields, our curriculum combines theoretical course work with hands-on, collaborative projects to help you apply data and information science concepts to real-world challenges.
Across both the two-year and four-year pathways, your core classes equip you to:
- Efficiently manage large datasets, including collecting, organizing and analyzing data to identify patterns and support evidence-based decisions.
- Implement cybersecurity fundamentals to protect information systems, assess risks and support secure digital environments.
- Design user-centered systems and digital experiences that make complex information accessible and usable.
- Transform complex data into clear visualizations and written insights that help organizations understand and act on information.
- Promote ethical technology usage by evaluating privacy, ethics and the broader cultural implications of emerging technologies and data use.
Visit the [Program Learning Outcomes](/isda-program-learning-outcomes) page for a comprehensive overview.
Careers
Data-driven insights power our world. A BS in Information Science and Data Analytics strengthens your ability to analyze data, design information systems and communicate insights, bridging the gap between technology and people.
From working with structured datasets to explaining the “why” behind the numbers, the professional development and hands-on training you receive prepare you to support data-informed decisions across industries such as technology, health care, education and business. Whatever your long-term goals, you can help organizations work smarter, move faster and protect the information that drives their success.
