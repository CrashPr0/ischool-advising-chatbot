ISDA 160 - User Experience (UX) Research and Design
3 unit(s)
Prerequisite(s): ISDA 130.
Grading: Letter Graded.
An overview of the concepts, principles, and methods of user experience (UX) research and user-centered design with a focus on inclusivity and equity. Students will design and implement UX studies, analyze results, and document and communicate findings and recommendations.
