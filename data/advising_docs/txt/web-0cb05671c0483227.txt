Your enrollment appointment is when you can start registering for classes online through
                  MySJSU—no in-person visit required. It will be posted on MySJSU about three months
                  before the semester starts and will end the day before the Enrollment Census Date.
                  New students will receive their appointments later than continuing students. See [Registration Calendar](/registrar/calendar/index.php) for more details. 
To view your enrollment appointment, login to your MySJSU, click "Academics: Enrollment" link, then click Enrollment Dates, your available terms will show in the middle window. See screenshot:
Registration start dates are based on [class level](https://catalog.sjsu.edu/content.php?catoid=14&navoid=5018#classification-of-students)(freshmen, sophomores, juniors or seniors) and priority registration policy. 
See [F17-4 Academic Senate policy \[pdf\]](/senate/docs/F17-4.pdf) for descriptions of priority students & registration groups.  
Students are grouped by class level, then priority groups, then last name groups. The order of the last name groups within the same class level rotates each semester.
All priority students and registration groups are approved by [the Student Success Committee of the](/senate/committee-taskforce-information/assignments.php#student-success) [Academic Senate](/senate/committee-taskforce-information/assignments.php#student-success). 
Class level is determined by a student's earned units and current work-in-progress.
