If you will complete your requirements before or later than term shown in MySJSU, follow the appropriate steps below. This process allows your evaluator to update your application for the correct term (if you are graduating early) or give you enrollment appointment(s) for subsequent terms if you will delay your graduation.

Graduate Students
Visit the GAPE site for details: https://www.sjsu.edu/gape/forms/index.php

Undergraduate Students
- All Business majors, submit the Service #1 Transaction Requests at the Jack Holland Student Success Center: https://www.sjsu.edu/lcobssc/
- College of Science (COS) students, use the COS-Graduation Date Change form: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/docs/GradDateChangeCOS.pdf
- All other majors, use this Graduation Date Change form: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/docs/GradDateChange.pdf

Instructions:
1. Download and Complete the Graduation Date Change form.
2. Check with your College Success Center for any internal requirements or procedures: https://catalog.sjsu.edu/content.php?catoid=10&navoid=630
3. If needed use DocuSign to collect required signatures: https://www.sjsu.edu/it/services/applications/docusign.php
4. Send the completed form directly to your graduation evaluator: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/evaluators.php

Additional Notes
- You will not receive enrollment appointments for any terms after the graduation term shown in MySJSU.
- If you have additional questions about your graduation status, please contact your evaluator.
