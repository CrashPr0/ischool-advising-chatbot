Information Science and Data Analytics, BS
Source URL: https://catalog.sjsu.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=23&poid=19100

The Bachelor of Science Information Science and Data Analytics (BS ISDA), offered by the School of Information, prepares students to work with data, technology, and people. BS ISDA majors attain skills that are applicable to a variety of careers involving analytics, visualization, big data, digital curation, digital assets management, knowledge management, information architecture, documents and records management, health care, education, and emerging technologies-all geared toward the information needs of users. Information Science and Data Analytics graduates are utilized by organizations to organize data and interpret the significance of data in a way that can be easily understood and used by others. They focus on what a person can do with an app rather than what an app does.

Program Delivery

The ISDA program is offered, in regular session as a 4-year degree program for freshman entering from high school or transfer students with fewer than 60 credits. In the Regular Session program, courses are delivered both on-campus and online. In general, the program is completed as a hybrid program.

Major Electives (12 units)
Major electives are offered within the ISDA program including ISDA 80, 81, 150, 160, 180, and 181. Other electives may be selected in consultation with an advisor.

Other electives may be selected in consultation with an advisor from approved courses outside the department or from approved graduate courses in the School of Information in areas such as Health Data Analytics, Enterprise Content Management and Digital Preservation, and Problem Solving with Data. If electives from the School of Information graduate courses are used to meet the elective requirements, the courses can only be used for the bachelor’s degree and cannot be later applied to any graduate degree.
