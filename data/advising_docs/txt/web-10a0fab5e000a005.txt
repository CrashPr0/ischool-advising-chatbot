Leave of Absence and Withdrawal
Office of the Registrar
San José State University

One semester: a student who has completed at least one semester at SJSU and is in good standing or on academic notice qualifies for a One Semester Leave without filing a leave request. International students should contact an ISSS advisor before taking that one-semester leave.

Two or more consecutive semesters: file a Leave of Absence Request before the next semester starts. The maximum leave is four semesters.

If currently enrolled, drop all classes via MySJSU by the deadline. If that deadline has passed, undergraduates submit the semester withdrawal request on the Undergraduate Education petitions page.
