View My Enrollment Appointments
- Go to one.SJSU -> Sign In using SJSU ID and Password.
- Select MySJSU.
- Navigate to Student Center > Enrollment Dates on the right side - Enrollment Dates Page displays.
- Select a term.
Note: If an enrollment appointment for your term is not listed, contact the Office of the Registrar. - Click Continue - Your enrollment appointments for the term you selected display.
