ISDA 100 - Technical Writing for Information
3 unit(s)
Prerequisite(s): LLD 100WB or COMM 100W or ENGL 100W or ENGL 100WB or ENGR 100W or MCOM 100W.
Grading: Letter Graded.
Introduces students to the principles of effective communication for technical, scientific, and business environments. Students will create written work in a variety of standard formats and use appropriate style to address the needs of targeted audiences.
