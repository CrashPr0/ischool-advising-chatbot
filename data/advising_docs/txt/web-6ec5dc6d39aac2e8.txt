Online Master of Library and Information Science
Lead the Future of Libraries, Archives and Information Services
In a world driven by information, the ability to connect people with knowledge has never been more important. The 100% online Master of Library and Information Science (MLIS) degree prepares you to help individuals, organizations and communities navigate an evolving digital landscape.
Learn how to lead in libraries, archives, data environments, information centers and emerging technology fields. Prepare not only to manage information, but to expand access, foster learning and create meaningful impact in the communities you serve.

What Can You Do With an MLIS Degree?
The MLIS prepares graduates for a wide range of careers across libraries, archives, museums, educational institutions, government agencies, nonprofits and private industry. Through flexible career pathways, students can tailor their degree to align with their interests and professional goals while building transferable skills that are in demand across the information profession.
In-Demand Skills: About 14,900 openings for librarians and library media specialists are projected each year through 2031.
Bay Area Salaries: MLIS professionals in the Bay Area earn a median salary of $70,000 - $100,000, according to Glassdoor.
