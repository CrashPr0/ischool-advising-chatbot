ISDA 121 - Human Centered Cybersecurity
3 unit(s)
Prerequisite(s): ISDA 20C and ISDA 20D.
Pre/Corequisite(s): ISDA 101.
Grading: Letter Graded.
Overview of human interactions with cybersecurity technology. How people react to cybersecurity policies and tools, reasons for behavior, development and evaluation of tools for assisting people in cybersecurity, human factors involved with social engineering attacks and phishing; ethical hacking.
