Online Bachelor's in Information Science and Data Analytics
Complete your degree 100% online with the same faculty, curriculum and accreditation as SJSU on-campus students.
Solve Complex Problems with Data and Technology
A Bachelor's Degree in Information Science and Data Analytics provides a foundation in the analysis, management, and interpretation of data, which are skills that are in demand at companies like Amazon, Adobe, Cisco, Google, Tesla and many more. Learn how to utilize data to make informed decisions about creating, replacing, improving or understanding information systems.

Out-of-State Eligibility
If you live outside of California, [check whether our program is approved to accept an application from you](/partners/state-authorization/index.php). 
Students from Oregon enrolled in an SJSU Online program will be subject to Oregon State requirements for transferring course works. Specifically, a grade of “C” or better must be earned in all General Education courses.
While our program allows out-of-state and international students to enroll and participate in courses remotely, please note that international students studying through our online program are not eligible to apply for an I-20 or a student visa. As you enjoy the flexibility of our online courses, please ensure you comply with your respective state or country's regulations regarding online education.

Valuable Learning Experiences
Our program features courses in data science, cybersecurity and user experience design as well as emerging topics such as big data, artificial intelligence, blockchain and AR/VR. Completing our program will enhance your ability to:
- Identify user-centric information and data needs of diverse populations.
- Demonstrate professional and ethical responsibility in data ownership.
- Understand how people from diverse backgrounds and cultures interact with technology and with each other in technological contexts.
Students in this program also receive free access to [Coursera Career Academy](https://ischool.sjsu.edu/coursera-career-academy), which includes online courses, micro-credentials and certificates from world-class

What Can You Do With an Information Science and Data Analytics Degree?
As a graduate of our bachelor’s program, you will have knowledge of software principles and practices, programming concepts and techniques, data structures and system development methods and practices. With this knowledge and skill set, you will be prepared for a career in data analytics, database administration, UX/UI, technology development, digital curation, cybersecurity, web content and development and information architecture.
Data Scientists in the Bay Area earn an average annual salary of $117,000 - $263,000, according to Glassdoor.
