Fall Graduation Deadlines
Graduate Admissions and Program Evaluations

The following degree deadlines are required to be met by graduate students graduating during the fall semester in December.

Fall Graduation Deadlines
Deadline: 04/01/2026
Requirement: Candidacy Form Submission to GAPE
Utilized by current students to record degree requirements.
Deadline: 09/01/2026
Requirement: Graduation Application (First-Time Applicants)
Students meeting graduation eligibility may apply via MySJSU.
Deadline: 09/01/2026
Requirement: Graduation Date Change Request
Utilized by students to change a graduation date to another term.
Important Note: View the College of Graduate Studies website for Thesis and Dissertation Review and Publication Due Dates.
For students completing a Plan A master's thesis or doctoral dissertation, review the College of Graduate Studies Thesis and Dissertation Information website for detailed instructions on how and where to submit each document.
Important Dates and Timelines
Please note, some of the following dates are subject to change in accordance with the University Academic Calendar.
07/29/2026: 1290R Registration begins for F-1 students. Student Employees (TAs) contact Dean's Office for 1290R appointment.
09/17/2026: 1290R Registration begins for remaining students.
12/07/2026: Last Day to Enroll in 1290R.
12/16-12/17/2026: Commencement Ceremony.
12/16/2026: Degree Conferral. Date listed on diploma/transcript confirming award of degree.
Jan-Feb: Graduation Review (Degree Posting). GAPE Evaluator reviews student file for degree completion.
Feb-Mar: Diploma Mailing.
