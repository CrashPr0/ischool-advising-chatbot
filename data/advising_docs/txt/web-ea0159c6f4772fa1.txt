Informatics Advising
First stop for MS in Informatics students in their advising journey is [Student Services](/student-services). They have the knowledge, expertise and experience to guide students to the correct resource based on individual needs.
Students can also interact with their peers on a variety of topics through the MS in Informatics advising site in Canvas. It is recommended each student review the [FAQs](/informatics-faqs) section for quick answers to common questions.
