Regular Session Students: Tower Cards
Tower cards are available at the Tower Card window 6 through 9 located in the Student Services Center, at 9th and East San Fernando streets (on the first floor of the parking garage). Present your driver’s license or other photo identification to have your picture taken.
For more information, see this Bursar’s office page, and see this list of frequently asked questions.
Special Session Students: Student ID
Only Regular Session students receive Tower Cards. However, the form of student ID that is available to Special Session students will qualify them for library services and the Microsoft Office software discount at the bookstore.
In order to obtain a Student ID card, after paying fees, Special Session students should:
- Send an e-mail to the Bursar’s office
- In the email, state that you are a Special Session student and want a paper ID card. You must provide:
- Name
- Mailing address (the address will be verified with the address on file in my.sjsu.edu)
- and Student ID number
The request for a Student ID card should not be submitted until after fees are paid.
Special Session Students near San José
Special Session students who come to the San José State campus can obtain a student ID card at the Tower Card window 6 through 9 located in the Student Services Center at 9th and East San Fernando streets (on the first floor of the parking garage).
