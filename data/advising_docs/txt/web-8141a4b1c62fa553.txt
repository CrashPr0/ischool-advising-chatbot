ISDA 141 - Information Visualization
3 unit(s)
Prerequisite(s): ISDA 140
Grading: Letter Graded.
Background of information visualization; perceptual and design principles of information visualization; data analysis methods and hands-on applications of visualization techniques as applied to structured and unstructured data; interaction and interface design issues; social visualization and visual analytics.
