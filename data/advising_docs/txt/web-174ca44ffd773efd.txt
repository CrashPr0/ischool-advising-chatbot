ISDA 150 - Topics in Information Science and Data Analytics
1-3 unit(s)
Prerequisite(s): ISDA 110.
Grading: Letter graded.
Note(s): Topics and prerequisites will vary by semester; may be taken a maximum of 3 times with different topics.
Current and emerging issues, practices, and technologies relevant to Information and Data Science professionals. Topics, which differ each semester, may include open source intelligence, XR technologies, or sports analytics
Repeatable for up to 9 units when content changes.
