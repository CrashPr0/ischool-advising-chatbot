ISDA 101 - Online Learning: Tools and Strategies for Success
1 unit(s)
Grading: Credit/No Credit/RP.
Note(s): Must be taken before or in the first semester of junior year; if unsuccessful must be retaken next semester; if failed a second time, student will be dismissed from the program.
Introduces students to a variety of successful strategies in the use of new and emerging technologies found in today’s online learning environment. Covers learning management tools, web conferencing, social software, university resources, skills for success in an online environment.
