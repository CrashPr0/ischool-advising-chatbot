MLIS Course Load
The Master of Library and Information Science degree program requires the completion of 43 units comprising:
- 
    [Six required courses making up
    16 units (INFO 200, 202, 203, 204, 285, 289 or 299)](/mlis-required-courses)
- 
    [27 units of electives](/mlis-electives)
The majority of courses offered in the MLIS program are three units. There are also some one-unit and two-unit courses, which provide increased scheduling flexibility and afford the opportunity to expand knowledge in a variety of areas.
MLIS students can expect to spend at least three hours outside of “class” per every course hour. The graduate-level course work is difficult. Students should carefully consider the program’s rigor and their other responsibilities outside of school, such as work and family obligations, when deciding on how many classes to take each semester. Dropping classes after the deadline early in the semester can be problematic and expensive. We recommend that students be conservative in the number of classes they take each semester.
Students can attend the MLIS program full time or part time and have up to seven years to finish their studies. San José State University does not apply credits earned more than seven years before graduation toward a master’s degree.
Students can earn their MLIS degree in as quickly as four semesters. MLIS courses are offered in the fall and spring semesters and over the summer break. Here are a few examples of how students can fast-track the fully online MLIS program.
