This site provides details about the graduation process and the support available from the Office of the Registrar.
Graduation evaluators review your academic record to ensure you meet all the requirements for your degree. This process is separate from the guidance you receive from your major advisor and success centers.

Application Central: See the graduation eligibility requirements, submission deadlines, application review process, and communication you can expect from our office. https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/application-central.php
Graduation Date Change: If you will complete your requirements before or after term shown in MySJSU, you must change your graduation date. https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/graduation-date-changes.php
Graduation Checklist: Step-by-step guide to confirm requirements, clear issues, and stay on track for graduation. https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/graduation-checklist.php
Graduation Notifications: Review your graduation status notification, based on your MyProgress report, to confirm whether you are on track to graduate or have outstanding degree requirements. https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/myprogress-graduation-status.php
Diploma Information: Review the details about your diploma, including how your name will appear, the printing schedule, and delivery methods. https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/diploma.php
FAQs About Graduation: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/faq.php
Graduation Evaluator: Locate your graduation evaluator. This person will review your file and award your degree when you complete all requirements. https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/evaluators.php
Graduation Tutorial: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/docs/GradAppOnlineTutorial.pdf
