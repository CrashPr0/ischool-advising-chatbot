Fall Payment Due Dates
Payment due dates are dependent on when the registration and housing charges are posted to a student’s account and are not necessarily posted at the same time. IPP refers to the Installment Payment Plan.
Fall 2026: Tuition and Fees Payment Due Dates
| Registration Period | Registration Charges Post to Account | Payment Due Date | IPP Due Dates | 
|---|---|---|---|
|  April 21 - Sept 11 | April 21 - Aug 11 | Aug 14 | Aug 14 Sept 14 Oct 15 Nov 14 | 
|  | Aug 12 - Sept 11 | Sept 14 | Sept 14 Oct 15 Nov 14 | 
- Last day to drop and receive 100% refund is August 18.
- If you register on September 12 or later, you have three days to pay from the date you register.
- No registration activity on September 16.
- Late add post census request requirement begins on September 17
