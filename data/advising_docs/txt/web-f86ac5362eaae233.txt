Your graduation evaluator is the person assigned to review your graduation application. This person will be your contact for all questions related to the graduation process including the review of your major, minor, graduation requirements and the awarding of your degree. Undergraduate students should use the lists below to locate their evaluator. Graduate students must visit the GAPE Site to find their evaluator: https://www.sjsu.edu/gape/connect/advisor-and-evaluator.php
