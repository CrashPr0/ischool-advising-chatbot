Replacement and Duplicates
You may place your diploma order online through our official diploma vendor, the Michael Sutter Company: https://michaelsutter.com/sjsu
Order a replacement diploma: There is a $10 fee for each diploma. Your diploma will be printed and mailed within three to four weeks of your order.
Order an Official eDiploma: An eDiploma is a signed and certified PDF of your original paper diploma.
After we print a diploma with the Diploma Name, students may order additional diplomas for a fee.

Holds on Your Account
Students with financial holds on their account will not receive their diploma. Check MySJSU to see if you have any holds. Inform your graduation evaluator when you have cleared your holds.
Source URL: https://www.sjsu.edu/registrar/graduation/diploma.php

Deadlines to add a Diploma Name, and the deadline to add or update the Diploma Address:
- Spring: June 1st
- Summer: August 15th
- Fall: January 5th
Students may use a name other than their Primary (legal) Name on their diploma. If the student adds a diploma name before the deadlines above, we will use that name on their diploma. Otherwise the diploma will show the Primary Name from MySJSU. The Diploma Name will be used exclusively for diplomas. Transcripts and official university records will continue to use the student's Primary (legal) Name.
