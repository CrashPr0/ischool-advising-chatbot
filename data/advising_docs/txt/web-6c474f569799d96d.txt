ISDA 170 - The Emerging Future: Technology Issues and Trends
4 unit(s)
Grading: Letter Graded.
Note(s): Should be taken during last semester of study.
In this capstone course students gain skills to plan strategically for the changing technological landscape. Students participate in forecasting activities through readings, video presentations, guest lectures, introductions to today’s futurists, and individual research.
