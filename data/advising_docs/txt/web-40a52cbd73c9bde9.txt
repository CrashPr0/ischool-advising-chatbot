Academic Requirements
To qualify for a bachelor’s degree, students must:
- Complete a minimum of 120 semester units
- Earn a cumulative grade point average (GPA) of at least 2.0
- Earn a GPA of at least 2.0 in all coursework completed at San José State University
- Earn a GPA of at least 2.0 in the major
- Earn a GPA of at least 2.0 in the minor, if applicable
- Complete all requirements established by the academic major
- Satisfy all university graduation requirements
In addition: A grade of C- or better is required in General Education Basic Skills courses and Ethnic Studies coursework when required by university policy. A grade of C or better is required in the Graduation Writing Assessment Requirement (GWAR). Additional minimum grade requirements may be established by academic programs.

Units in Residence and Upper-Division Unit Requirements
To earn a bachelor’s degree, students must complete:
- A minimum of 30 units in residence at San José State University after matriculation
- At least 24 upper-division units completed in residence
- At least 12 upper-division units in the major completed in residence
- At least 40 upper-division units overall
For Bachelor of Arts degrees, at least 12 upper-division units must be completed in the major. For Bachelor of Science degrees, at least 18 upper-division units must be completed in the major. Only coursework recognized as upper-division by university policy may be applied toward upper-division unit requirements. Additional residence requirements may apply to specific academic programs.

Applying for Graduation
Students are responsible for applying for graduation by published university deadlines. Students should normally apply for graduation approximately two semesters before their anticipated graduation term. Application for graduation initiates a formal review of degree progress and graduation eligibility. Students remain responsible for completing all outstanding degree requirements even after submitting a graduation application.

Degree Conferral and Commencement
Degrees are formally conferred following verification that all graduation requirements have been completed. The degree conferral date reflects the academic term in which degree requirements are satisfied. Participation in commencement ceremonies does not constitute conferral of a degree. Commencement ceremonies are celebratory events recognizing academic achievement and may include students who have completed degree requirements as well as students who are expected to complete requirements in an upcoming term, as permitted by university policy. Official degree conferral occurs only after all requirements have been verified and approved by the university.
