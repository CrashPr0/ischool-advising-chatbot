Graduation marks the completion of your degree requirements. Commencement is the celebration of that achievement.

What is "Graduation"?
Graduation is the process of completing your required courses to obtain your diploma at the end of your tenure at an academic institution. When you complete your requisite courses and earn your required units towards a degree, your coursework and grades earned are reviewed and certified so that you can be permitted to be awarded a degree.

What is "Commencement"?
Commencement is the physical celebration most people associate with "graduations": speeches, stage-crossing, tassel-moving, photography with diploma frames and the wearing of academic regalia.
At San José State University, nine colleges hold Commencement ceremonies every fall and spring semester to celebrate the academic graduation of cohort of Spartans and to award their earned degrees.
