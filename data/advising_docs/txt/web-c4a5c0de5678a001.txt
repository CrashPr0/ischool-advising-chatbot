Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS)
Student Wellness Center
San José State University

CAPS offers counseling for SJSU students. Same-day support can be in person or over Zoom. Drop in, or call 408-924-5678, during business hours to schedule. CAPS also offers self-scheduled same-day telehealth appointments that become available each morning, Monday through Friday, in the SWC Patient Portal.

After business hours, follow the emergency and crisis page. For urgent mental health support after hours, call 408-924-5678.
