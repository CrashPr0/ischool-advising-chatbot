Roadmap Notes
*Courses marked with an asterisk may be taken in a different semester than listed.
Lower division courses should generally be taken in years 1-2, and upper division courses in years 3-4. Students must have completed 60+ units in order to enroll in Upper Division GE courses. Students should work with an academic advisor to create an individual roadmap using the MyPlanner tool.
[1] Both courses are recommended if students wish to later pursue a Master of Science in Data Analytics.
[2] Courses must be passed with a C- grade or better.
Upper or Lower Division Electives (12 units)
Electives can be selected in consultation with an advisor from upper or lower division courses outside the department, or from approved graduate courses in the School of Information in areas, such as Health Data Analytics, Enterprise Content Management and Digital Preservation, Digital Curation, Digital Assets Management, Digital Forensics, Crisis/Disaster Informatics, Problem Solving with Data. If electives from graduate courses in the School of Information are used to meet the elective requirements, the courses can only be used for the bachelor’s degree and cannot be later applied to any graduate degree.
A minimum of 120 units is required for this degree.
