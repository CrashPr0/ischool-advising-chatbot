Payment Due Dates
Students are responsible for keeping track of payment dates. Conveniently, payment
                  due dates are posted in the finances section of your [MySJSU](https://one.sjsu.edu) account immediatelly after you register for classes. For step-by-step instructions,
                  see [How to View Payment Due Dates \[pdf\]](/bursar/docs/steps/view_due_date.pdf). 
Payment due dates also posted online and updated right before each semester's registration period. Select the appropriate semester below:
Open University Students
Open University payment due dates are different. Visit the [Open University calendar](https://sjsu.edu/professional/openuniversity/fall-spring/calendar.php) for payment due dates or email [Professional and Continuing Education (PaCE)](/professional/resources/) at [pace@sjsu.edu](mailto:pace@sjsu.edu?subject=).
Special Session Students
Special Session payment due dates depend on your enrollment date. Check your due dates
                  in [MySJSU](https://one.sjsu.edu) or email [Professional and Continuing Education (PaCE)](/professional/resources/) at [pace@sjsu.edu](mailto:pace@sjsu.edu?subject=).
