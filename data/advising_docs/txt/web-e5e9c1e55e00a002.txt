Emergency and Crisis Support
Student Wellness Center
San José State University

If the situation is life-threatening, call 911 immediately or go to the nearest emergency room.

For urgent mental health consultations, call or text 988, the Suicide and Crisis Lifeline.
https://988lifeline.org/

After-hours emergencies: urgent medical or mental health support, call 408-924-5678.
https://www.sjsu.edu/wellness/access-services/emergency-crisis.php

SJSU University Police: if you are experiencing an emergency, call 911, or call the University Police Department at 408-924-2222 on any phone.
https://www.sjsu.edu/police/
