Informatics Course Load
The MS in Informatics program requires the completion of 30 units. The following sample scenarios of different course loads may be helpful for planning. There is no requirement to take courses according to one of these specific sample scenarios. Be sure to consult the [Course Rotations](/informatics-course-rotations) and [Career Pathways Calendar](/node/4545) pages to plan your schedule and Career Pathway.
Fall Start – 6 units per session – Health Career Pathway
Fall Session 1
- INFM 200: Informatics Fundamentals (3 units)
- INFM 201: Informatics Technology Foundations (2 units)
- INFM 202: Information Security Overview (1 unit)
Fall Session 2
- INFM 203: Big Data Analytics and Management (2 units)
- INFM 204: Human Centered Design (2 units)
- INFM 205: Informatics Project Management (2 units)
Spring Session 1
- INFM 206: Electronic Records Foundations (3 units)
- INFM 207: DAM-Digital Assets Management (2 units)
- INFM 208: Information Security-Information Assurance (1 unit)
Spring Session 2
- INFM 210: Health Informatics (3 units)
- INFM 213: Epidemiological Methods (3 units)
Summer Semester
- INFM 211: Informatics Culminating Project (3 units)
- INFM 214: Health Data Analytics (3 units)
